Courses Offered & Well-Qualified Teachers

Coding

We teach students how to make personal websites. Coding is the process of writing step-by-step instructions in a programming language to tell a computer what tasks to perform.

Robotics

We teach students how to create machines and robots. Robotics is an interdisciplinary field dedicated to the design, construction, operation, and application of robots.

AI Literacy

We teach students how to use laptops and prompt AI effectively. Artificial Intelligence is the ability of a computer system to perform tasks that typically require human thinking, reasoning, and problem-solving.

Animation

We teach students how to animate videos. Animation means giving life to still images by displaying sequential frames rapidly to simulate movement.
